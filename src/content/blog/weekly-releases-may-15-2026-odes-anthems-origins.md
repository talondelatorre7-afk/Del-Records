**Some songs capture a moment, others define a lifetime.** This week’s releases from the DEL Records family tap into the fundamental human drivers that shape our stories. We have a timeless ode to beauty, a pure-energy anthem for the perfect night, and a narrative reflection on where it all began. From personal devotion to collective celebration, these three tracks prove that the biggest hits are powered by the most personal feelings.

## How Does Martín Beltran Define 'Bella'?

![Bella Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/ab04abfa-77a2-4b8f-aa28-81777d83bf69/2eb2e3bd-e1ab-458b-a936-efcdd0f5e18c.jpg)

Martín Beltran delivers “Bella,” a track that feels both classic and contemporary - a heartfelt serenade for the modern age. The aong is a masterclass in devotion, moving beyond simple compliments to capture the kind of admiration that sees beauty in everything a person does. With his characteristically smooth and emotive vocal delivery, Beltran makes a powerful declaration that is impossible to ignore.

The production is lush and romantic, creating a warm soundscape that allows the sincerity of the lyrics to take center stage. “Bella” isn’t just a song, it’s a feeling - the sonic equivalent of locking eyes across a crowded room and knowing, without a doubt, that you’re witnessing something special. It’s a timeless sentiment, impeccably delivered.

> "No hay joya en el mundo, ni estrella en el cielo, que brille tan fuerte como tú."

**Released:** May 15, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

## What Night Is El Rabbanito Getting Ready For?

![I Gotta Feeling Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/cd784862-a3a3-4fd8-a8db-c7398aa78ee7/f24186ff-81fa-419f-9c87-19bf7b18eb28.png)

Borrowing its title from one of pop’s most iconic phrases, El Rabbanito’s “I Gotta Feeling” is a bold reinterpretation rather than a straight cover. He takes the familiar anticipation of a big night and reshapes it in his own voice, delivering the track in his unique version of English with a delivery that feels personal, unfiltered, and unmistakably his.

What makes this version stand out is the tempo. El Rabbanito slows the song down to roughly half-speed, trading the original’s frantic urgency for something more deliberate and atmospheric. The result lets every line breathe, turning a phrase the world already knows into a slow-burning, unapologetic statement piece carried entirely by his distinctive vocals.

> "La música suena, la noche nos llama, y siento en el alma que todo va a estar bien."

**Released:** May 15, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "I Gotta Feeling" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/igottafeeling)

## What Did T3R Elemento Dream Of 'Desde Niño'?

![Desde Niño Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/a8a55aa0-0a72-4cb4-b35b-cbb02ebf7601/99f0e305-4caf-41e7-baf9-2f5ca4c4ce89.jpg)

T3R Elemento brings their signature storytelling prowess to “Desde Niño,” a powerful track that connects the past to the present. The title says it all - “Since I Was a Kid” - and the song unfolds as a compelling narrative of ambition, hustle, and the dreams that fuel a journey. It’s a reflective look back at the foundations of a life, delivered with the raw authenticity that fans have come to expect.

This is more than just a biography set to music. It’s a statement of identity, tracing a direct line from childhood aspirations to present-day reality. The track is a testament to perseverance, celebrating the vision it takes to turn youthful dreams into a tangible career. With grounded production that lets the lyrical narrative shine, T3R Elemento adds another essential chapter to their ever-expanding musical legacy.

> "Desde niño sabía que este era mi camino, con los pies en la tierra y la vista en el destino."

**Released:** May 15, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "Desde Niño" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/desdenino)

## The Week in Review

From the focused admiration of Martín Beltran’s “Bella,” to the collective celebration of El Rabbanito’s “I Gotta Feeling,” and the foundational storytelling of T3R Elemento’s “Desde Niño,” this week’s releases explore the core forces that shape our lives. Whether looking at another person, looking forward to the night, or looking back at the past, the DEL Records roster once again proves its unmatched ability to capture life’s most defining moments in song.
