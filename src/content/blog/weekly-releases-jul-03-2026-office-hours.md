## What's Inside LA DIFERENCIA's 'Bolsitas de Coricos'?

![Bolsitas de Coricos Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/17962a59-3a95-4623-a027-4e1cdc58925d/553a00de-fd69-4905-a0e7-d978f0b1c502.png)

LA DIFERENCIA makes a statement with "Bolsitas de Coricos," a track whose title hints at a story deeper than meets the eye. Referencing the traditional Sinaloan cookies, the song uses this regional touchstone as a clever metaphor for commerce and street-level enterprise. It’s a narrative delivered with the quiet confidence of someone who knows their product and their worth.

The production is tight and focused, creating a soundscape that feels both authentic and assertive. This isn't just a song - it's a business plan set to music. LA DIFERENCIA lives up to their name by carving out a unique lane where clever lyricism and grounded storytelling come together to paint a vivid picture of the modern grind.

**Released:** July 3, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

## Who's Taking Calls in El Rabbanito's 'Oficina'?

![Desde la Oficina Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/1a9cf525-29cf-48be-a6ac-19d6cdce8a14/6435a3fb-3381-4e4a-8578-0c101a4cf850.png)

El Rabbanito delivers a boss’s anthem with "Desde la Oficina." Forget the cubicle and the corporate ladder - this "office" is a command center for a self-made operation. The track pulses with the energy of ambition, positioning El Rabbanito as the one making the calls, managing the flow, and running the entire show from behind the desk.

With a cool, calculated delivery, he outlines the perspective of a leader who has built something from the ground up. The track serves as a motivational soundtrack for anyone charting their own course, reminding listeners that the most powerful headquarters is the one you build yourself. It’s a confident, swaggering declaration of independence and success.

**Released:** July 3, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "Desde la Oficina" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/desdelaoficina)

## What Happens When Yahir Saldivar & Luis R. Conriquez Hit 'Las 4'?

![Las 4 Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/889.jpg)

It’s the collaboration fans have been waiting for. The viral force behind the cumbia bélica movement, Yahir Saldivar, joins forces with the undisputed king of corridos bélicos, Luis R. Conriquez, for the event track "Las 4." The title itself - 4 AM - evokes a specific time when the party peaks, the night shifts, and the most important moves are made in the quiet of the early morning.

This single is more than just a song; it's a cultural moment. It bridges two massive waves within Regional Mexican music, blending Saldivar's infectious, danceable energy with Conriquez's raw, commanding presence. "Las 4" is destined to own the late-night playlist, a powerful anthem about what happens when the rest of the world has gone to sleep but the work - or the celebration - is far from over.

**Released:** July 3, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records
