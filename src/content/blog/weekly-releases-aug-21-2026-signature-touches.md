**Every artist has their medium. For some, it's a brush and canvas. For others, it's a guitar and a microphone. This week's DEL Records releases explore both sides of the creative coin. Emanuel Garcia returns to deliver his signature artistic statement, a sound uniquely his own, while Diana Araujo presents a blank canvas - a vulnerable, beautiful space ready for a new story to be painted. It's a week about vision, style, and the art of making music that feels personal.**

## How Does Emanuel Garcia Define His 'Toque'?

![CON MI TOQUE 3 Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/bee2637e-eafa-4e7f-a21c-f10bbbdc4bee/f21e9380-8fe2-4511-af10-63c3ef40334d.png)

Emanuel Garcia continues to build his legacy with **“CON MI TOQUE 3,”** the third installment in a series that has come to define his artistic identity. The title itself is a declaration of confidence: “With My Touch.” This isn't just a collection of songs - it’s a sonic signature, a project that solidifies Garcia’s unique approach to the genre. Each track feels hand-crafted, stamped with the unmistakable flair that fans have come to expect.

Following the success of the first two volumes, “CON MI TOQUE 3” feels less like an experiment and more like a masterclass. The production is tight, the delivery is assertive, and the overall mood is one of an artist fully in command of his craft. Garcia isn’t just participating in the conversation - he’s shaping it with his own distinct voice and style.

**Released:** August 21, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "CON MI TOQUE 3" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/conmitoque3)

## What Picture Does Diana Araujo Paint on 'LIENZO'?

![LIENZO Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/17c17238-1c6c-4263-af52-fbfeabcbe04a/b6838ab8-349a-4854-81ea-8477346eaa93.png)

Where Emanuel Garcia’s release is about a defined signature, Diana Araujo’s new single, **“LIENZO,”** explores the beauty of a fresh start. The title, which translates to “Canvas,” is a perfect metaphor for this breathtakingly vulnerable track. Araujo positions herself as a blank slate, a space ready to be filled with the color and emotion of a new love story. It’s a powerful and deeply romantic concept.

The song’s magic lies in its quiet strength. Araujo’s vocal performance is filled with a soft, hopeful intimacy, allowing the listener to feel the weight of every potential brushstroke. “LIENZO” is not just a love song - it’s an invitation. It’s about the promise of what could be, the thrill of a new beginning, and the courage it takes to hand someone the brush and trust them to create something beautiful.

**Released:** August 21, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "LIENZO" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/lienzo)

## The Week in Review

This week, DEL Records showcases the duality of artistry. Emanuel Garcia delivers a project built on the confidence of a well-defined style, while Diana Araujo offers a song rooted in the vulnerability of a blank canvas. One is a statement of identity, the other an invitation to create one. Together, they prove that whether the artist is making their mark or providing the space for a new one, the results can be equally stunning. **Two artistic visions, one label.**
