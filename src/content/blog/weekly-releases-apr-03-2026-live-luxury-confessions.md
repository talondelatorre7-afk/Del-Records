**This week's DEL releases capture the full spectrum of the artist experience - from the roar of a live audience to the aspirational flex of international success, anchored by the internal questions we can't escape. It's a slate that celebrates both the massive statement and the intimate confession, proving that true artistry lies in mastering both.** Whether it’s the raw energy of a live set or the quiet complexity of a three-way collaboration, this is music that feels as big as a stadium and as personal as a private thought.

## How Does LA DIFERENCIA Capture Lightning in a Bottle?

![100% En Vivo Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/2b67c0ca-3733-42e7-a91c-be62b418992d/d42f8704-3661-4bb1-abdb-5ba60fd68107.png)

There's studio magic, and then there's the undeniable, unfiltered energy of a live performance. LA DIFERENCIA bottles that electricity with **"100% En Vivo,"** an album that does exactly what it says: delivers pure, authentic stage presence directly to your speakers. This project isn't just a collection of songs; it’s a snapshot of a moment where the band and the audience become one. It’s the sound of passion, improvisation, and the powerful connection that only happens in real time.

Capturing a live album is a high-wire act - there are no second takes, no studio tricks, only the shared feeling in the room. LA DIFERENCIA proves they thrive in that environment, turning in a performance that's both technically tight and emotionally expansive. With an entire visualizer playlist live on YouTube, fans can now see and hear the magic for themselves. It’s a must-listen for anyone who misses the feeling of the front row.

## What Does Success Sound Like for Emanuel Garcia & Sucesión M?

![SANTIAGO BERNABEU Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/2850568c-8356-4497-9b7d-d7a746c4ed1c/448f7b5e-0d07-4785-9132-bbd7f09e8c04.png)

Emanuel Garcia and Sucesión M team up for **“SANTIAGO BERNABEU,”** an unapologetic anthem celebrating the spoils of success. The track is a catalog of the high life - diamond watches, G-Wagons, trips to see Real Madrid play - and a testament to seeing dreams materialize. It's a modern corrido that trades dusty trails for designer storefronts and international travel, all while remembering the barrio they came from.

The collaboration is seamless, blending two confident flows over a beat that feels as luxurious as the lifestyle it describes. This isn’t just a flex; it's a motivational speech set to music, a reminder that hard work pays off in very tangible, very stylish ways. It’s a track that knows exactly what it is and invites you to enjoy the view from the top.

> Viendo el Real Madrid en el Santiago Bernabeu
> Hago lo que quiero al final es mi dinero

**Released:** April 03, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "SANTIAGO BERNABEU" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/santiagobernabeu)

## Who Are Jason Cota, CUERNO & Alannis Trying to Explain To?

![Como Le Explico Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/d4db5d8e-d6a3-498b-b8dd-56bfeeedeec6/4242e8f6-d29a-4782-acda-3c23ee81db73.png)

The most powerful questions are often the hardest to answer. On **“Como Le Explico,”** three of DEL’s most compelling voices - Jason Cota, CUERNO, and Alannis - join forces to explore that very territory. The title itself, translating to “How do I explain it,” hangs in the air, full of unspoken emotion. It’s a song built for the space between words, for the feelings that are too tangled to easily unravel.

Bringing these three artists together suggests a story with multiple perspectives. Is it a tangled love affair? A confession? A quiet moment of self-reflection? The magic is in the mystery. Cota’s measured storytelling, CUERNO’s heartfelt delivery, and Alannis’s soulful presence promise a track that is emotionally rich and deeply relatable. This is a collaboration that feels less like a feature and more like a necessary conversation.

**Released:** April 03, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "Como Le Explico" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/comoleexplico)

## What Statement Is Cobian Montana Making?

![DE LA C HASTA LA M Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/2608fb15-32a9-4539-a158-0a999b815935/d23dab65-495f-4dd8-9ad3-ab2f54c8d21b.jpg)

Cobian Montana arrives with **"DE LA C HASTA LA M,"** a track whose title alone is a bold statement of identity. Using an initialism instantly creates an air of confidence and insider knowledge - a flag planted firmly in the ground. This isn't just a song title; it's a brand, a code, and a declaration all in one.

The track is poised to be an anthem of self-definition. In a musical landscape crowded with voices, Cobian Montana chooses to lead with a title that is both definitive and intriguing. It’s the kind of move that signals an artist who is clear in his vision and ready to command attention on his own terms. Get ready to learn what it means to go from C to M.

**Released:** April 03, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

## The Week in Review

From the raw connection of a live show to the polished gleam of global success, this week’s lineup is a powerful showcase of the DEL Records roster. LA DIFERENCIA brings the heat of the stage, Emanuel Garcia and Sucesión M provide the soundtrack for the victory lap, while the trio of Jason Cota, CUERNO, and Alannis dive into emotional complexity. Closing with Cobian Montana’s bold self-declaration, the message is clear: whether shouted from a stage or whispered in confidence, these are stories that demand to be heard.
