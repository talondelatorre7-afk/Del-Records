**There’s a world of difference between asking to be seen and arriving so everyone has to look.** This week’s DEL Records releases explore both sides of that journey. One is an intimate, urgent demand for recognition, a plea to look closer. The other is a victory lap with the windows down - a rolling testament to ambition fulfilled. Together, they paint a picture of the hustle and the reward.

## What Do Andi Luan & Misael Beltran Want You To See In "Mira"?

![Mira Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/7b2d4b9d-818a-49aa-839b-26125af6de5a/45fd4343-0b18-498b-89d0-c4dd33e1e38d.png)

In the powerful new duet **“Mira,”** the title is not a suggestion - it is a command. **Andi Luan ♛** and **Misael Beltran** join forces for a track built on the tension of being overlooked. It’s a compelling collaboration that pairs Andi Luan’s regal, commanding presence with the seasoned storytelling in Beltran’s voice, creating a conversation that feels both intimate and intensely dramatic.

The production wisely stays out of the way, allowing the raw emotion of the performance to take center stage. This isn’t a song about passive longing; it’s an active demand for the attention and validation one deserves. It’s the sound of someone drawing a line in the sand, tired of waiting for their value to be recognized and deciding to claim it for themselves.

> "Mira, que el tiempo no perdona... mira, que el futuro está en mis ojos."

**Released:** August 14, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "Mira" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/mira)

## Where is El Rabbanito's "La Cadillac" Taking Us?

![La Cadillac Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/6fa35d96-4e06-4142-a30f-8a83fed6637c/3e2c7bf7-5f8a-49f3-89ef-5e3b81179c4b.png)

From the tense intimacy of “Mira,” we shift gears and hit the open road with **“La Cadillac.”** **El Rabbanito** teams up with **Clemente y su Elexion Privada** for a track that feels like the destination after a long journey. The Cadillac here isn’t just a car - it’s a trophy, a rolling symbol of success that announces your arrival before you even step out.

This is a celebratory anthem through and through, steeped in the classic sounds of success and confidence. The track captures the feeling of a victory lap - the satisfaction of cruising through streets that once witnessed your struggle. The collaboration is seamless, creating a full-bodied sound that’s perfect for turning up loud and enjoying the ride. It’s a song about rewarding yourself, a reminder that the hard work pays off in polished chrome and the respect of your peers.

> "Quemando llanta en la Cadillac... el volante en mis manos, el mundo a mis pies."

**Released:** August 14, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "La Cadillac" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/lacadillac)

## The Week in Review

This week, the DEL Records roster charts a course from vision to victory. Andi Luan and Misael Beltran demand that you look and see the future in their eyes, while El Rabbanito proves what happens when you do - you end up behind the wheel of your own success story. One track is the plea, the other is the proof. Stream both now to experience the full journey.
