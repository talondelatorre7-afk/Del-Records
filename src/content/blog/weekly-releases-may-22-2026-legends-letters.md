**This week, the DEL Records roster explores the moments that define us. From the suspended animation of a love in limbo to the triumphant roar of a goal achieved, these four tracks capture pivotal turning points. They are songs about standing on a threshold: between wanting and forgetting, between history and legend, between doubt and certainty. Here's what's new.**

## What Happens When Love Lives Between Wanting and Forgetting?

![ENTQMQY Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/dfec83ad-d1ad-4c56-9ec9-848fb5654652/31dcb7bf-12de-48df-a0f5-8ddecaf5996c.jpg)

CUERNO returns with "ENTQMQY," a track whose title - an acronym for "Entre que me quieres y me olvidas" - perfectly encapsulates the emotional whiplash of a relationship on the edge. It's a song built for the space between messages, for the nights spent wondering where you stand. CUERNO channels the frustration and magnetism of a connection that is both undeniable and uncertain.

Here, love isn't a destination; it's a tightrope walk. The production mirrors this tension, creating a soundscape that feels modern, anxious, and deeply relatable. It captures the feeling of being pulled in two directions at once, a push-and-pull that is as intoxicating as it is maddening.

> "Entre que me quieres y me olvidas, se me va la vida esperando una señal."

**Released:** May 22, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "ENTQMQY" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/entqmqy)

## How Does El Rabbanito Frame the "Leyenda Caro Quintero"?

![Leyenda Caro Quintero Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/cb7ccf96-bde7-454d-ae1b-a73d84f6391c/944306f8-3dd8-4af8-8a31-fdb5c1b94caa.png)

El Rabbanito adds another chapter to the rich tradition of the corrido with "Leyenda Caro Quintero." This is storytelling in its purest form, a track that steps into the larger-than-life narrative of a historic figure. Rather than focusing on romance or personal struggle, El Rabbanito wields his voice to paint a portrait of power, legacy, and myth.

The track is a testament to the enduring power of the corrido to document and mythologize. With a powerful vocal delivery and classic instrumentation, El Rabbanito honors the genre's roots while proving its continued relevance. It's a bold musical statement that adds a different, more narrative-driven texture to this week's releases.

> "Se escribe la historia con tinta y con plomo, dejando un legado que el tiempo no borra."

**Released:** May 22, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "Leyenda Caro Quintero" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/leyendacaroquintero)

## What Does Osvaldo Zambrano Want to Know in "Quiero Saber"?

![Quiero Saber Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/02a09424-9b00-4569-b0ce-e42dc0288dcd/17baf61c-8e65-4219-af11-0346c70b7b30.png)

Osvaldo Zambrano has a knack for capturing the quiet vulnerability that follows a heartbreak, and "Quiero Saber" is a masterclass in the art of the unanswered question. Following songs of raw pain, this track shifts into the next phase: the search for closure. It is a direct and honest plea for the truth from a heart that is tired of guessing.

Zambrano’s performance is hauntingly sincere. He isn't angry or bitter - he's simply searching for a final piece of information to help him move forward. The song's power lies in its universal theme. Who hasn't been left wondering what was real and what was imagined? "Quiero Saber" is the anthem for anyone who just needs to know.

> "Quiero saber si alguna vez fui de verdad, o solo un eco en tu silencio."

**Released:** May 22, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "Quiero Saber" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/quierosaber)

## What Does Success Sound Like on LA DIFERENCIA's "Lo He Logrado"?

![Lo He Logrado Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/941c4f96-df8a-4a03-8439-2a079d8a8e40/ee762876-6b66-48b7-b83d-a364e5e77d87.png)

Closing out the week on a wave of pure triumph, LA DIFERENCIA drops "Lo He Logrado." This is a victory lap in musical form - a powerful, feel-good anthem dedicated to anyone who has ever overcome obstacles to reach their dreams. The track radiates confidence and the deep satisfaction that comes from hard-won success.

With its driving rhythm and celebratory energy, "Lo He Logrado" is poised to become the soundtrack for graduations, promotions, and personal milestones everywhere. It's a song about looking back at the struggle with pride and facing the future with an unshakeable sense of accomplishment. LA DIFERENCIA has crafted a universal ode to achievement.

> "Mirando atrás, veo el camino andado, y con orgullo digo: lo he logrado."

**Released:** May 22, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "Lo He Logrado" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/lohelogrado)

## The Week in Review

From the narrative sweep of a corrido to the intimate plea for answers, this week's lineup showcases the full spectrum of human experience. El Rabbanito tells the stories of legends, while CUERNO and Osvaldo Zambrano grapple with the legends of their own hearts. And LA DIFERENCIA provides the perfect closing statement: a celebration of writing your own successful ending. **Four releases, four defining moments, all under one iconic label.**
