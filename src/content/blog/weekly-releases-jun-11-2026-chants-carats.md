**Some releases whisper, others demand to be heard. This week, the DEL Records family delivers two tracks that fall squarely in the second category - songs built around undeniable presence. From the raw, crowd-moving energy of a stadium chant to the timeless brilliance of a flawless gem, these artists know how to command a room. Get ready for a week of pure, uncut energy.**

## What's the Unstoppable Beat Behind Yahir Saldivar's "Chiquitibum"?

![Chiquitibum Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/0236f899-f5aa-4f11-82fc-0b65bf223223/86cdeb79-2a30-4f7a-af69-b813011d9a33.jpg)

"Chiquitibum" is a title that crackles with sound before you even press play. Yahir Saldivar bottles that raw, percussive energy and unleashes it in his latest single for DEL Records. It’s a track built for high-volume playback, driven by a rhythm that feels both primal and perfectly engineered for the dance floor.

The song operates on pure momentum, capturing the spirit of a chant that unites a crowd. It’s less about complex narrative and more about a shared feeling - an explosive burst of confidence and unfiltered fun. Saldivar delivers a performance that feels immediate and infectious, making "Chiquitibum" an instant contender for the summer's most high-energy playlists.

**Released:** June 11, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "Chiquitibum" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/chiquitibum)

## Why is DSEIKO & Jason Cota's "DIAMANTE CARO" So Unbreakable?

![DIAMANTE CARO Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/37714386-1a18-4601-87ed-edde5d3c8645/5e1bb55f-6a7b-4579-99f1-8d93bf229c55.png)

Following the high-octane energy of "Chiquitibum," DSEIKO and Jason Cota shift the focus to a different kind of power: quiet value. "DIAMANTE CARO" translates to "Expensive Diamond," and the track lives up to its name with a polished, confident sound that explores themes of self-worth and irreplaceability.

The collaboration is a study in texture. DSEIKO and Jason Cota trade verses, creating a dynamic that feels both smooth and street-smart. The production is clean and modern, giving their vocals space to shine - much like a diamond against black velvet. It's a song about recognizing value, whether in yourself or in someone else, and refusing to settle for anything less than flawless.

**Released:** June 12, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "DIAMANTE CARO" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/diamantecaro)

## The Week in Review

This week at DEL Records was all about making a statement. Yahir Saldivar brought the noise with an anthem that's impossible to ignore, while DSEIKO and Jason Cota delivered the shine with a collaboration that glows with confidence. One track is the chant, the other is the carat - and both prove that true presence never asks for permission.
