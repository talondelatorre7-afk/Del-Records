**Four fresh tracks. Four different flavors of love.** This week's DEL releases run the full emotional spectrum - from the thrill of a secret connection to the innocent dream of a white dress, with a breezy collab and a raw heartbreak anthem filling in between. Whether you're catching feelings or catching flights, there's something here for every mood.

## Hadrian - "FOTOS"

![FOTOS Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/909.jpg)

Hadrian opens the week with **"FOTOS,"** an unapologetically confident track that lives in the tension between what's deleted and what's saved. The premise? Those photos in her phone - the ones Face ID unlocks, the ones her man doesn't know about.

> "Llevo noches sin dormir... tengo fotos de nosotros, baby, no las puedo borrar"

The production rides a dark, moody wave built around jealousy, memory, and the weight of a phone gallery full of things you can't let go of. Hadrian leans into the emotional push-and-pull, moving from late-night invitations to the kind of nostalgia that keeps you scrolling at 2 AM. It's magnetic, confident, and delivered with the swagger of someone who knows the phone always rings back.

**Released:** February 12, 2026 · **Label:** TRKZ X DEL, LLC.

## Sucesión M & Golden Ganga - "Viento"

![Viento Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/f28a0f5f-1c60-498c-9694-3ece16ba9d58/aa66265d-12c0-48e8-8e13-66f4d935a06b.jpg)

**Sucesión M** links up with **Golden Ganga** for "Viento," a collaboration that brings together two distinct sounds under the DEL SLG Music umbrella. The track carries an effortless, breezy energy - the kind of song that soundtracks a sunset drive with the windows down.

This pairing is a natural fit, blending melodic flow with laid-back vibes that let the production breathe. Sometimes the best releases are the ones that don't try too hard, and "Viento" hits that sweet spot.

**Released:** February 13, 2026 · **Label:** DEL SLG Music, LLC

[Stream "Viento" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/viento)

## CUERNO - "Vestido Blanco"

![Vestido Blanco Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/910.jpg)

After the energy of "FOTOS," CUERNO takes us in a completely different direction with **"Vestido Blanco"** - a love letter wrapped in a wedding vow. The track paints a picture of a secret admirer who sent flowers to her parents' house, building up the courage to finally make it official.

> "Vestido blanco en el castillo dorado mi amor, te quité el velo, una luna de miel en besos"

There's a genuine tenderness here that's refreshing. CUERNO doesn't pretend to be perfect - *"Sé que no soy el más guapo, rico, o romántico, pero mi corazón late por ti"* - and that honesty is exactly what makes the track land. It's the kind of song that plays at the wedding AND at the proposal.

**Released:** February 13, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "Vestido Blanco" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/vestidoblanco)

## Osvaldo Zambrano - "CON MI ❤️"

![CON MI CORAZON Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/897.jpg)

Closing out the week on a bittersweet note, **Osvaldo Zambrano** delivers "CON MI ❤️" - a raw confession from someone still processing the exit wound. She left without a goodbye, without explanation, and now he's stuck scrolling through what used to be.

> "Ya he tratado con varias y no quise a ninguna... solo jugabas con mi corazón"

The beauty of this track is its simplicity. No elaborate metaphors, no theatrical production - just an honest admission that moving on isn't as easy as downloading a new app. Zambrano captures that specific kind of heartbreak where you're not angry anymore, just confused. *"Me duele... ya he tratado y no puedo olvidarla."* We've all been there.

**Released:** February 13, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "CON MI ❤️" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/conmicorazon)
