**Time isn't always linear. Sometimes it stops, spins, or lines up perfectly.** This week's DEL Records releases dive deep into those critical moments. One track captures the dizzying free-fall of emotional vertigo, while the other seizes the power of a single, opportune moment. It's a study in timing - the kind that can either throw you off balance or change your life forever.

## What Does Jason Cota Feel at the Edge of 'VÉRTIGO'?

![VÉRTIGO Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/5df9fe45-b678-46e7-97d1-a00365cd4d58/e9290b7e-0b23-4e45-b748-16d233f5215a.png)

Jason Cota kicks off the week with **"VÉRTIGO,"** a track that perfectly captures the feeling of standing on an emotional ledge, unsure whether to jump or step back. The title itself promises a sense of disorientation, and Cota delivers, using the physical sensation of vertigo as a powerful metaphor for the intoxicating chaos of falling in - or out - of love. It’s a feeling of losing control, where the world spins and the only thing holding you steady might be the very thing making you dizzy.

Cota has a knack for bottling raw, unfiltered emotion, and "VÉRTIGO" is a masterclass in atmospheric tension. The production swirls around his voice, creating a soundscape that feels both vast and claustrophobic. It’s a track that doesn’t just describe a feeling; it immerses you in it, pulling you into the whirlwind of a heart that's lost its equilibrium. The result is a compelling, high-stakes anthem for anyone who has ever felt beautifully and terrifyingly off-balance.

> "En el borde de tus ojos, pierdo el equilibrio y no sé quién soy"

**Released:** July 24, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "VÉRTIGO" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/vertigo)

## What Opportune Moment Defines Martin Beltran's 'Kairos'?

![Kairos Cover Art](https://lebnvuttuycnaulnojmn.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/release-images/4e6d9f88-c7d7-4b23-a7a3-3ab52a263d5c/1ca3a437-a335-4eae-82cf-e420b4d80c90.jpg)

In stark contrast to the chaos of "VÉRTIGO," Martin Beltran's new single **"Kairos"** is a testament to clarity and perfect timing. The title draws from the ancient Greek concept of an opportune, defining moment - not just any time, but the *right* time. Beltran uses this idea to craft a song about destiny, purpose, and the life-altering power of a single moment when everything aligns.

Where Cota’s track is a dizzying spin, Beltran's is a confident step forward. "Kairos" is built on a foundation of decisive energy, with crisp production and a vocal performance that exudes self-assurance. It’s the soundtrack to recognizing a window of opportunity and having the courage to climb through it. This isn't a song about waiting for fate; it's about meeting it halfway, recognizing the signs, and seizing the moment that was always meant for you.

> "Este es el momento, el tiempo se detuvo para que cruzáramos caminos"

**Released:** July 24, 2026 · **Label:** DEL Records

[Stream "Kairos" on all platforms →](https://delrec.io/kairos)
