Nobody saw this coming.

"Todo Lo Fue" sat as track 10 on Lenin Ramírez's *Reinicio* album for almost two years with no radio push, no features, no fanfare. Then in late January 2026, TikTok happened.

[youtube:kwwdY1bp-Ck]

The sierreno ballad exploded to **6.2 million YouTube views** in just eight days, a staggering **532x** the DEL Records channel's normal performance. On TikTok, it hit **#1 in Mexico** and **#8 in the U.S.**, soundtracking countless videos about complicated love and "almost" relationships.

Spotify's algorithm caught on fast. The track landed on Viral 50 Global, Viral 50 Mexico, and the flagship *La Reina* editorial playlist. Total streams have now passed **35 million**.

What makes this breakout remarkable is how organic it was. WhatsApp drove 24% of the video's traffic. People texting the song to friends in private chats. YouTube search accounted for 28% as listeners hunted down the track after hearing it elsewhere. This wasn't a label push. It was word-of-mouth at scale.

Written by Jesús Chairez (who also penned Lenin's multi-Platinum "Recordando a Manuel"), "Todo Lo Fue" captures a universal feeling: loving something that felt like everything but was never officially anything at all.

For Lenin Ramírez, this is the latest chapter in a decorated career spanning 15+ years and nearly 80 releases. His 2019 collab "Yo Ya No Vuelvo Contigo" with Grupo Firme hit #1 on Billboard's Mexico Popular Airplay and racked up **750M+ YouTube views**. His album *Bendecido* won Regional Mexican Album of the Year at the Billboard Latin Music Awards.

"Todo Lo Fue" proves that in the streaming era, there's no expiration date on a great song. Nearly two years after release, the right moment arrived, and millions of new listeners found their way in.

## March 11, 2026 Update

Six weeks in, "Todo Lo Fue" is not just holding. It is still climbing.

The official YouTube video has now surpassed **43.5 million views**, nearly doubling the 24.4 million reported just two and a half weeks ago. Watch time has reached **1.8 million hours**, and the track has driven **+9,700 new subscribers** to the DEL Records channel.

The view graph tells a striking story: sustained daily volumes of **800,000 to 1.2 million+ views** from late January through mid-March, with no significant drop-off. This is not a spike followed by a fade. It is a sustained hit performing at massive scale for over six weeks straight.

At this pace, "Todo Lo Fue" is on track to cross **50 million views** before the end of March. What began as a TikTok rediscovery has become one of the defining regional Mexican releases of early 2026.

## February 23, 2026 Update

The Billboard charts have taken notice. "Todo Lo Fue" debuted at **#3 on Billboard Mexico Songs**, climbing from #5 the prior week in just its third week on the chart. It marks one of the highest-charting entries for a DEL Records track in recent memory.

On YouTube, the official video has now surpassed **24.4 million views**, crossing the **1 million watch hours** milestone. The numbers continue to defy expectations:

- Views are running **1,100 times higher than usual** for the DEL Records channel
- The video has driven **+6,200 new subscribers** to the channel
- In the last 48 hours alone, the video pulled **2.46 million views**

The traffic profile has shifted significantly since the early breakout days. Playlists now drive **59%** of all views, up from the WhatsApp and search-driven organic discovery that fueled the initial surge. YouTube search accounts for **14.4%**, with browse features and suggested videos each contributing around **9%**.

The playlist-driven growth signals something important: "Todo Lo Fue" has moved beyond viral discovery into sustained, algorithmic momentum. Listeners aren't just finding it once. They're adding it to rotation.

## February 18, 2026 Update

"Todo Lo Fue" is still accelerating. Three weeks after the initial breakout, the official YouTube video has now surpassed **17.4 million views**, with the growth curve showing no plateau.

Updated YouTube analytics:

- **745,700 hours of watch time** since publish
- Views are running **888 times higher than usual** for the DEL Records channel
- The video has driven **+4,800 new subscribers** to the channel

The view count alone has nearly doubled since the February 10 update, jumping from 10 million to 17.4 million in just eight days. More subscribers are choosing to watch it, and they're watching it longer, helping to increase its reach on YouTube recommendations.

## February 10, 2026 Update

The viral momentum behind "Todo Lo Fue" shows no signs of slowing down. Since its breakout on January 28, 2026, the official YouTube video has now crossed **10 million views** - nearly doubling the 6.2 million milestone reported just days after the initial surge.

The YouTube analytics tell the full story:

- **430,200 hours of watch time** accumulated since publish
- Views are running **671 times higher than usual** for the DEL Records channel
- The video has driven **+3,200 new subscribers** to the channel

What started as a TikTok-fueled rediscovery has become one of the biggest organic hits in regional Mexican music this year, and it's still climbing.
